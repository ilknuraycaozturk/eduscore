# Trust-First Business Model & Revenue Strategy

**Document Code:** EDU-BIZ-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Prepared By:** EduScor Product Office
**Date:** 15 July 2026

# 1. Purpose

Defines EduScor's monetization strategy while preserving the platform's most valuable asset: **user trust**.

**Trust must always be more valuable than short-term revenue.**

# 2. Vision

EduScor will become the world's most trusted Education Intelligence Platform by ensuring that commercial activities never compromise recommendation quality, transparency or user confidence.

# 3. Core Business Principles (non-negotiable)

- Trust Before Revenue — Revenue generation must never reduce platform credibility.
- Transparency Before Profit — All paid content must be clearly identified.
- Recommendation Independence — Recommendations shall never be influenced by commercial payments.
- Long-Term Brand Value — Long-term user trust is more valuable than short-term advertising income.

# 4. Growth Strategy

## Phase 1 — Build Trust
Primary objective: Acquire users. Revenue target: Not a priority.
Success indicators: Active users, Returning users, Searches, School comparisons, Saved favourites, User satisfaction.

## Phase 2 — Sustainable Revenue
Revenue begins after user trust has been established. Primary customers: Educational institutions (not parents).

## Phase 3 — Platform Economy
Examples: Education consultancy, Learning services, Online education marketplace, Professional development, AI premium services.

# 5. Monetization Roadmap

**Stage 1 — Free Platform:** School search, comparison, profiles, reviews, favourites, basic recommendations. No mandatory payment.

**Stage 2 — Verified School Programme:** Schools may subscribe for a verified badge after identity/profile-accuracy verification. Verification does **not** improve rankings.

**Stage 3 — Enhanced School Profiles:** Additional photographs, promotional videos, virtual campus tours, rich media. Presentation only — does **not** affect recommendation results.

**Stage 4 — Sponsored Visibility:** Clearly labelled "Sponsored", never mixed with organic recommendations, never influences EduScor Score.

**Stage 5 — Marketplace Services:** Educational consulting, tutoring, STEM products, books, events, learning materials. Commission-based.

**Stage 6 — Premium Membership:** Unlimited comparisons, saved searches, personal education profile, advanced reports, future AI assistant. Core school discovery remains free.

**Stage 7 — AI Premium:** Introduced only after sustainable operational maturity. Models: monthly subscription, usage credits, institutional licensing.

# 6. Revenue Sources

Verified School subscriptions, Enhanced School Profiles, Sponsored placements, Premium memberships, Marketplace commissions, Enterprise services, API licensing (future), AI subscriptions.

# 7. Activities Explicitly Prohibited

EduScor shall never:
- Sell school rankings.
- Sell EduScor Scores.
- Accept payment to improve recommendation results.
- Publish fake reviews.
- Remove legitimate criticism in exchange for payment.
- Hide sponsored content.
- Manipulate comparison results for commercial benefit.

These rules are **permanent platform policies**.

# 8. Trust Framework

Every recommendation must be: Transparent, Explainable, Independent, Evidence-based, User-first.

# 9. Trust Statement

> "No school can buy a higher EduScor Score or a better recommendation."

Permanent platform commitment.

# 10. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

**Growth KPIs:** MAU, DAU, Search volume, Comparison volume, Returning visitor rate, User retention, School profile views.

**Trust KPIs:** Verified review ratio, User trust score, Recommendation acceptance rate, Complaint resolution time, Transparency compliance, Sponsored content disclosure compliance.

**Revenue KPIs:** Verified School subscriptions, Premium memberships, Marketplace GMV, ARPU, CAC, LTV.

# 11. Long-Term Strategic Position

EduScor is **not** an advertising platform, **not** a lead generation website, **not** a ranking business.
EduScor is an **Education Intelligence Platform** whose commercial model is built upon trusted educational guidance.

# 12. Strategic Conclusion

Revenue should be a consequence of trust — not a substitute for it.
