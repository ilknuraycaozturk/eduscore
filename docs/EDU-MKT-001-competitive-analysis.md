# Global & Türkiye Competitive Analysis and Market Positioning

**Document Code:** EDU-MKT-001
**Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** Draft | **Date:** 15 July 2026

# 1. Executive Summary

The education discovery market is highly fragmented (school listings, rankings, parent reviews, university search, online course marketplaces, career guidance). No widely adopted platform combines trusted education data, decision support, school comparison, lifelong learning and future AI-assisted recommendations into a single ecosystem. EduScor aims to occupy this gap.

# 2. Global Market Overview

**K-12 School Discovery:** Niche, GreatSchools, SchoolDigger (USA) — school profiles, rankings, parent reviews, basic comparison. Limitations: country-specific, limited personalization, no explainable recommendation engine, AI not central.

**University Discovery:** QS Top Universities, THE, Studyportals, Peterson's, Unigo — rankings, admission info, scholarships. Limitations: institution-focused rather than learner-focused, limited end-to-end decision support.

**Online Learning:** Coursera, Udemy, edX, FutureLearn, LinkedIn Learning — course marketplace, certifications. Limitations: don't help choose schools, not integrated with formal education discovery.

**Career & Education Planning:** Naviance, Scoir — career guidance, university planning. Limitations: closed ecosystems, institutional use, limited public discovery.

# 3. Türkiye Market Overview

**School Discovery:** Okul.com, özel okul platformları, MEB sayfaları, bireysel okul siteleri. Strengths: directories, contact info, promotional content. Limitations: inconsistent data quality, limited comparison, limited personalization, fragmented info, minimal decision support.

**University Information:** YÖK Atlas, üniversite tanıtım siteleri, tercih dönemi rehber platformları. Strengths: official data, programme info. Limitations: focused on higher ed only, limited full-journey guidance.

**Online Education:** BTK Akademi, Bilgeİş, üniversite uzaktan eğitim merkezleri, private providers. Limitations: independent ecosystems, no unified learner profile, no connection to school selection.

# 5. EduScor Positioning

Positioned as an **Education Intelligence Platform**, not a traditional school directory. Pillars: Trust, Transparency, Explainability, Structured educational data, User-centric decision support, Scalable architecture.

# 6. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

> "Helping learners and families make confident education decisions through trusted information, transparent comparisons and intelligent guidance."

# 7. Market Gaps

Fragmented education information; limited cross-stage journey support; weak comparison capabilities; minimal personalization; lack of explainable recommendations; no unified education profile spanning school, university and lifelong learning.

# 8. SWOT Analysis

**Strengths:** Broad product vision, AI-ready architecture, scalable geographic model, strong documentation foundation.
**Weaknesses:** Early-stage product, limited brand awareness, initial dataset must be built.
**Opportunities:** Growing demand for digital decision support, expansion into multiple education segments, international scalability, future AI-assisted services.
**Threats:** Established directory platforms, search engine dependency, data maintenance effort, regulatory changes.

# 9. Initial Geographic Strategy

Pilot: Bağlıca. Expansion: Etimesgut → Ankara → Türkiye → International.

# 10. Strategic Conclusion

EduScor is not intended to compete solely as a school listing website. Its strategic objective is to become a trusted Education Intelligence Platform supporting education decisions throughout the learner lifecycle.
