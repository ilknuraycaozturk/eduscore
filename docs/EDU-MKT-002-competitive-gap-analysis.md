# Competitive Gap Analysis — Global & Türkiye Education Platform Market

**Document Code:** EDU-MKT-002
**Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** Draft | **Date:** 15 July 2026

# 1. Purpose

Identifies functional, strategic and UX gaps in current education discovery platforms (global & Türkiye), so EduScor focuses on unmet user needs rather than replicating existing products.

# 2. Executive Summary

Current platforms perform well at presenting information but provide limited decision-making support. EduScor's opportunity: evolve from an **Education Directory** into an **Education Decision Platform**.

# 3. Strategic Market Gap

Current Market: Platforms help users **search**.
EduScor: EduScor helps users **decide**.

# 4. Major Competitive Gaps

- **GAP-01 — Information Exists, Decision Support Does Not:** platforms rarely answer "which school is the best fit for this specific learner, and why?" → EduScor provides structured, explainable decision support.
- **GAP-02 — Fragmented Information:** users combine search engines, school sites, social media, forums, messaging groups → EduScor becomes the single trusted hub.
- **GAP-03 — Rankings Without Context:** users don't understand scoring criteria/weighting → EduScor recommendations are transparent and explainable.
- **GAP-04 — Generic Recommendations:** most platforms recommend the same schools to everyone, ignoring learning style, special needs, interests, sports, arts, transport, budget, languages, philosophy → EduScor prioritizes personal compatibility over popularity.
- **GAP-05 — No Decision Journey:** platforms stop after search results → EduScor guides: Need → Priorities → Budget → Location → Educational expectations → Comparison → Decision.
- **GAP-06 — Limited Trust in Reviews:** authenticity, moderation transparency, promotional bias concerns → EduScor builds trust through transparent moderation and clear policies.
- **GAP-07 — Limited Transparency:** users can't distinguish organic vs sponsored vs editorial → sponsored content always clearly labelled.
- **GAP-08 — Weak Comparison Tools:** typical comparisons focus on specs; families actually compare suitability, long-term outcomes, financial impact, daily experience, advantages, risks → EduScor compares outcomes, not just attributes.
- **GAP-09 — Fragmented Education Journey:** preschool/primary/secondary/university/online/professional development treated as separate ecosystems → EduScor supports the full lifecycle.
- **GAP-10 — No Lifelong Learning Perspective:** most platforms end after school selection → EduScor accompanies: School → University → Certificates → Professional education → Career development.
- **GAP-11 — No Decision Memory:** platforms rarely remember previous searches, eliminated schools, preferences, comparison history → future: persistent education decision profile.
- **GAP-12 — Limited Local Intelligence:** neighbourhood-level guidance uncommon (Bağlıca, Yaşamkent, Çayyolu, Bilkent, Ümitköy) → EduScor starts with deep local coverage before expanding.
- **GAP-13 — Incomplete Cost Visibility:** families typically see tuition only, not transportation/meals/uniforms/books/clubs/trips/registration/fees → EduScor supports total cost visibility.
- **GAP-14 — Lack of Explainable Recommendations:** users deserve to understand "why this school?" (e.g. close to home, within budget, strong robotics programme, tennis facilities, English-focused curriculum, high compatibility with priorities).

# 5. EduScor Strategic Position

Competitors answer: "Which school ranks highest?"
EduScor answers: "Which school is the best fit for this learner, and why?"

# 6. Product Principles Derived from the Analysis

EduScor shall:
- Support decisions rather than searches.
- Prioritize transparency.
- Explain every recommendation.
- Build trust before monetization.
- Begin with one region and scale gradually.
- Support lifelong learning.
- Remain AI-ready while keeping AI optional in early stages.

# 7. Key Strategic Insight

The greatest opportunity is not providing more information — it's helping families and learners make better decisions using trusted, transparent and structured educational intelligence.
