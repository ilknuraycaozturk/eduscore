# 1. Executive Summary

EduScor is an AI-ready Education Intelligence Platform designed to help parents, students, professionals and educational institutions make informed education decisions through trusted, structured and explainable information.

The first product release (MVP) will focus on building a reliable education information platform without Artificial Intelligence. AI capabilities will be introduced after the platform achieves sustainable user growth and revenue.

# 2. Business Need

Education decisions are among the most important decisions individuals make, yet information is fragmented across websites, social media, forums and institutional pages.

Users currently face challenges such as:

- Information overload
- Unverified reviews
- Lack of standardized comparisons
- Difficult school discovery
- Limited transparency
- Time-consuming research

EduScor aims to centralize educational information into a single trusted platform.

# 3. Project Purpose

Develop a scalable digital platform that enables users to discover, compare and evaluate educational opportunities using structured information and, in later phases, AI-assisted recommendations.

# 4. Vision

To become the world's most trusted Education Intelligence Platform.

# 5. Mission

Empower every learner and every family to make confident education decisions using trusted information, transparent comparisons and intelligent technology.

# 6. Strategic Objectives

The project aims to:

- Build the first EduScor MVP.
- Validate market demand.
- Acquire the first active users.
- Build a trusted education database.
- Prepare the platform for future AI integration.
- Establish a scalable product architecture.

# 7. Business Objectives

Short-term:
- Launch MVP.
- Cover the first pilot region.
- Validate user experience.

Medium-term:
- Expand geographically.
- Introduce premium services.
- Increase user engagement.

Long-term:
- Become the leading education platform in Türkiye.
- Expand internationally.
- Introduce AI-powered personalized guidance.

# 8. Project Scope

Included:
- Public education information
- School profiles
- Search
- Filtering
- School comparison
- User registration
- Favorites
- Reviews
- Responsive web platform
- Administrative management panel

Excluded from MVP:
- AI assistant
- Mobile applications
- Blockchain components
- International education coverage
- Advanced analytics
- Paid memberships
- AI-generated recommendations

# 9. MVP Scope

The first public release will include:
- Homepage
- Search
- School listing
- School details
- Comparison
- User accounts
- Favorite schools
- Reviews
- Basic administration

Pilot Region: **Bağlıca (Ankara)**

# 10. Geographic Expansion Strategy

- Phase 1: Bağlıca
- Phase 2: Etimesgut
- Phase 3: Ankara
- Phase 4: Türkiye
- Phase 5: International

# 11. Success Criteria

- MVP is publicly available.
- Users can search and compare schools.
- Initial user feedback is collected.
- Platform operates reliably.
- Architecture supports future AI integration.

# 12. Assumptions

- Open-source technologies will be used whenever practical.
- Initial development budget will be minimized.
- Publicly available information can be used in accordance with applicable laws and platform terms.
- AI functionality is intentionally postponed until later phases.

# 13. Constraints

- Limited initial budget.
- Small founding team.
- Gradual geographic rollout.
- Web-first strategy.
- AI postponed from MVP.

# 14. Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data quality | High | Data validation process |
| Limited funding | High | Zero-cost-first approach |
| Slow user adoption | Medium | Continuous user feedback |
| Regulatory changes | Medium | Periodic legal review |
| Data maintenance effort | High | Standardized data governance |
